# Original work and research

The garment silhouette, ribbed collar, seam paths, fold shading, fabric pattern, layout, and After Hours moth study were authored for SELVEDGE. No garment photographs, competitor UI/assets, brand marks, customer artwork, or private files were used. Gallery screenshots are generated from original or synthetic test data.

The initial product research considered apparel visual tooling alongside other independent-product themes. Apparel offered a convincing visual surface and a useful zero-API workflow. [ThreadCam's public site](https://www.threadcam.com/) already describes browser studio tooling, color sets, placement, and print exports; these are not claimed as new inventions here. SELVEDGE explores coordinated collections, revision comparison, and a clear reviewable handoff with original implementation and identity.

The parent's September 30, 2026 research snapshot reported [ThreadCam on TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/threadcam) at $7,523 MRR and $2,242 over the prior 30 days. That is neighboring reported demand, not audited financial evidence, a forecast for SELVEDGE, or a revenue claim about this project. No monetization infrastructure is implemented.

## Bundled dependencies and assets

- [Instrument Serif](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/instrumentserif): original regular TTF, SIL OFL 1.1. License bundled at `public/fonts/InstrumentSerif-OFL.txt`.
- [DM Sans](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/dmsans): original variable TTF, SIL OFL 1.1. License bundled at `public/fonts/DMSans-OFL.txt`.
- Both authored fonts are used for Latin text; Russian/Kazakh have explicit Georgia/Times New Roman and Arial/system fallback paths, checked in browser screenshots and layout tests.
- [Lucide](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide): ISC-licensed icons supplied through `lucide-react`.
- [React](https://github.com/facebook/react), [Vite](https://github.com/vitejs/vite), and [jsPDF](https://github.com/parallax/jsPDF) retain their respective package licenses. Installed versions are fixed by `package-lock.json`.

Hosting guidance follows [GitHub Pages custom workflows](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/using-custom-workflows-with-github-pages) and [GitHub's additional-product terms](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-terms-for-additional-products-and-features). The public deployment is an open-source portfolio demonstration.
